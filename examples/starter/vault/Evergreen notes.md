Evergreen notes are written to be revised and linked over years rather than finished once. Each one tries to hold a single idea, titled as a claim.

They get their value from [[ideas/Backlinks|backlinks]]: a note you wrote last year turns up again when something new links to it.

## Titles as claims

A title like "Spaced repetition makes memory a choice" is easier to link to from a sentence than "Spaced repetition".
