---
title: Backlinks
date: 2026-10-06
---

A link only points one way. A backlink is the same link seen from the other end: a list, on each note, of every note that points to it.

Without them, a note can only lead outward. With them, it also shows where it's been used, which is how [[Evergreen notes|evergreen notes]] accumulate context. See [[Evergreen notes#Titles as claims]] for why linkable titles help.
